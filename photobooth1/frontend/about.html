Why this exists

Photobooths are usually a shared, physical moment between two people, one booth, one strip of film. Photogether tries to keep that same feeling when the two people aren't in the same place.

Create a room, share the code, and take a photo strip together over live video, from wherever you both are.

Ps. This is a small, ongoing project so expect rough edges while it's being built out.



How to Use Photogether

Follow these simple steps to create a virtual photobooth session with people you want to take photos with.


01. Create a Room

Click Create Room on the homepage.

A unique room code will be generated for you. This code is what the other person will use to join your photobooth.

Tip: Send the room code to the person you want to take photos with.


02. Join the Room

If you're the other person, click Join Room.

Enter the room code that was shared with you, then click Join.

Once both people are inside the same room, your cameras will appear side by side.


03. Allow Camera Access

When your browser asks for camera permission, choose Allow.

Before continuing, make sure:

  • Your camera is working.
  • Your face is visible in the preview.
  • Both people have joined the same room.

You can also enable the microphone if you want to use audio during your session.


04. Adjust Your Camera

Before taking photos, use the camera controls to get your shot ready.

You can:

  • Mirror your camera.
  • Rotate your camera.
  • Position yourself however you like.

Each person can adjust their own camera independently.


05. Choose the Number of Shots

The host can choose how many photos will be taken.

Select between 1 and 6 shots.

Once you're ready, the host can press Start.


06. Get Ready for the Countdown

A 3-second countdown will appear before each photo.

Get into position, pose, and have fun!

A flash effect will appear when the photo is captured. The process repeats until all selected shots are finished.


07. Customize Your Photo Strip

After all photos have been taken, you'll see your finished photo strip.

Choose from the available frames and designs to change its appearance.

You can also open the Photo Editor to customize your strip further, including repositioning and editing your photos.


08. Save Your Memories

When you're happy with your photo strip, click Save & Download.

Your finished photo will be downloaded to your device.

Keep it, print it, or share it with the person you took it with.
