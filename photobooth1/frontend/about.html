Why this exists

Photobooths are usually a shared, physical moment — two people, one booth, one strip of film. Photogether tries to keep that same feeling when the two people aren't in the same place. Create a room, share the code, and take a real photo strip together over live video, from wherever you both are.

This is a small, ongoing project — expect rough edges while it's being built out.
